# How ubsite is built

What is built, feature by feature: the modules and tables of each part, and how it works. Until 2026-10-26 this was §3 of CLAUDE.md, which keeps the layout of the tree and a map of this file. Why each thing is as it is, and who chose it, is in `DECISIONS.md`. A § or a trap number names `CLAUDE.md`'s.

## Listening

Since 2026-10-07. Every section can be heard in Merritt Horn's reading, and from any paragraph once his reading is aligned:

- `build/audio.py` reads `data/audio/` into `audio_source`, `audio_file`, `audio_segment` and `audio_unread`. Each voice is a list of segments, `(file, start_ms, end_ms)`, one per paragraph plus a spoken heading. The player never knows whether a voice is one file per section or one per paragraph.
- `build/speech.py` builds the `speech` table: the text as a voice is to read it, with numbers in words, list marks spoken and brackets dropped. It is gated so that no digit or symbol survives.
- `tools/align/` turns the recordings into `data/audio/mh.tsv`: Whisper, difflib, and cuts placed in pauses.
- `tools/tts/` makes the AI voice: the listening test that chose it, and `make tts` (below, Where the recorded AI reading stands).
- `/audio/<key>/<name>` serves only names listed in `audio_file`, with Range support.
- **Your browser's voice** (2026-10-08) needs no recording: the browser speaks the `speech` text itself (the Web Speech API), fetched from `/speech/<paper>:<section>` once that voice is chosen. It is a stopgap beside Merritt Horn until the recorded AI reading exists, then a third choice (`DECISIONS.md`, and below).
- The player is `static/listen.js` (~37 KB, section pages only). A paragraph's ▶ starts it there, the first paragraph's from the spoken heading, and the voice is changed in the bar as well as in Settings (`DECISIONS.md`). While it plays with Follow along off, it also follows the site's links inside the page, so the reader can go anywhere and the reading goes on (`DECISIONS.md`). Settings, the ⚙ dialog, are declared in `ubsite/settings.py`, run by `ub.js`, stored in `localStorage` under `ub_settings`, and applied before first paint (`DECISIONS.md`).

## The text API

Since 2026-10-11. `/api/v1` lets other sites keep their copy of the English text in step with ours, and `/api` documents it, with a client in Python.

- **The protocol is a text version.** A site keeps the number it was sent and asks `/api/v1/text?since=N`. It gets every record new or changed since N, the keys of any removed, and the new number. From 0 it gets the whole text, 3 MB gzipped.
- **A record** is a part, a paper, a section or a paragraph, each with a hash. A paragraph also has `html`, `uf_ref`, `page_1955`, `separator_before`, `group_before`, `kind`, `setoff` and `readings_1955`. The `html` is `render.py`'s, with an empty `<span class="v1955">` for each 1955 reading in place of the site's note link.
- **`build/versions.py`** keeps the versions in an append-only ledger, `data/text/`, because `ub.db` remembers no earlier build. The ledger is appended only after a build passes.
- **`build/foundation.py`** gives every paragraph and section the Foundation's reference, `uf_ref`. It reads its SRT HTML (the zip in the press repo) and aligns it with ours paper by paper. Its differences must be exactly `tests/known-uf-refs.tsv`.
- **`ubsite/api.py`** only concatenates stored records. Each worker gzips a reply once. Replies carry an ETag, so an unchanged one is a 304, and `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`.
- **Calls are counted apart from views**, in `api_call` in `visits.db`. `/stats` lists each caller by its User-Agent.
- **A passage or a paper by reference** (2026-10-13): `/api/v1/paragraphs/<refs>` and `/api/v1/papers/<numbers>` send the same stored records, in reading order, each once, with the section, paper and part around them. `api.py` `_book` places every paragraph in reading order once per worker, a selection becomes runs of places (`_merge`), and `_select` caches each reply by its runs, so equivalent selections share a reply and an ETag. The text version does not move.

## The 1955 readings and their reasons

Since 2026-10-12. Each `\fnc` is a small ring above the words that changed; a click opens a card under the line with the 1955 reading **as the press gives it** and the **SRT Committee's decision and reasons**. The margin holds the index alone. **Only the press says what 1955 printed** (Tigran, 2026-10-12): the committee's page has errors of its own, in quoting 1955 among them (trap 21), so it supplies its reasons, under its name, and nothing else. Its quotation of the 1955 text pairs a note with its entry and is never shown.

- `tools/srt/snapshot.py` (`make srt-snapshot`) keeps the two tables of the Fellowship's page (`sources.toml [srt]`) in `data/srt/considerations.html`, verbatim, with the URL, fetch time and page hash in its header. The build reads the snapshot, never the network, and records it in `build_source` as `srt`.
- `build/srt.py` reads it into `srt_item`, one row per change (an entry may hold several: 134:3.3 has three), maps the page's references (the Foundation's numbering) to ours through `uf_paragraph`, rewrites its links to our paragraphs, and pairs each note with its change (`footnote.srt_item_id`). What does not pair one to one is exactly `tests/known-srt-deltas.tsv`.
- `section.html` puts the notes after the text as endnotes (what a reader sees without JavaScript, and in print); `ub.js` opens each as a card at its ring, positioned in `<article>` so it scrolls with the text. `ubsite/queries.py` decides what a note shows.
- **Search finds them too** (Tigran, 2026-10-12), by the reading and by the reasons its card shows: `footnote_fts` (`004_fts.sql`, filled by `derive.fts`), one row per note, never the committee's quotation of 1955 and none of its entries that have no note. `/search` lists the hits as "In the 1955 readings": the reading, today's words around the ring, the committee's words where they matched, and a link, `/93:5.8#note93_5_8_0`, that lands on the paragraph with the card open (`#note`, not the ring's own `#fnref`, which the browser would scroll to itself, over `ub.js`). The list is always sent and shown only when Settings' "1955 readings in search results" is on (off by default), so no setting reaches the server. 48 readings hold a word today's text never uses: *Badanon*, *Tannach*, *forego*, *commerical*.
- **The List of 1955 readings**, `/lists/1955` (Tigran, 2026-10-12): all 180 by paper, each with its reading, today's words around the ring, and the committee's verdict. Its rings are the text's own `a.fn` and open the same card in place, and so do the rings among the search results: `ub.js` frames a card in `main.results` where there is no `<article>`. A note is selected once, by **`queries.notes()`**, for a section, the list and a search, and drawn once, by `templates/_note.html`; a list line is `templates/_old.html`. The list keeps its rings with the readings turned off in the text, because a reader asked for it.

## Justified, hyphenated where the book hyphenates

Since 2026-10-12. The text is set justified, and a word breaks at a line's end only where the press lets it. `build/hyphen.py` runs the press's own model: Knuth's `hyphen.tex` (vendored from TeX Live, `vendor/tex/`), hyphenmins 2/3, the 523 entries of `tex/urantia-hyphen-en.tex`, and the source's 1,392 `\-`, which the expander now keeps as zero-width `shy` spans. `paragraph.html_hy` is `paragraph.html` with a U+00AD at each of those points, and only the section page uses it. `html` and the API are byte-identical to before, so the text version did not move.

- `build/checks/check_hyphen.py` gates it: removing every U+00AD gives `html`; every `\-` is kept; nothing breaks under the minimums; every listed word breaks as its entry says.
- `tools/hyphen_print.py` holds it to a built PDF of the book. On 2026-10-12 all 23,494 breaks printed in words of the text were offered, against 1,217 missed with the patterns alone. It is not a gate, because the PDF can be stale (CLAUDE.md §5).
- Ragged right is a setting (`DECISIONS.md`). Chrome copies soft hyphens, so `ub.js` takes them out of the selected text for the moment of a copy and puts them back before the next paint. The copy is then the browser's own: plain text and HTML, with its styles, byte-identical to a copy from the page without them (checked 2026-10-12: one paragraph, three with the margin between, part of a word, red letter with a 1955 ring, dot-leader rows, verse, the whole page).

## Reading paths

Since 2026-10-13. `/i/<slug>/read` reads a headword's citations as one text: every paragraph it cites, once, in the order of the book, with the entries citing it beside it; `/i/<slug>/read/theme` reads the same paragraphs a chapter to each of the index's level-2 groups. A concept page links its path as "Read it through", with its length.

- `derive.reading_path` builds `reading_path`: a row for each A–Z headword with a path (`PATH_MIN`, 3 paragraphs: 2,058 of 4,736), with its paragraphs, words and chapters. A headword has a path if it has a row.
- **`queries.reading_path()`** arranges it, ported from the mockup's generator: running heads where the section changes, a fold for what a section has between two paragraphs drawn, a one-line pointer back for a paragraph drawn above (By theme only), a signpost for a paper cited whole, verse grouped as `queries.section` groups it. Captions follow the margin's rule: a range at its first paragraph only (`opens`).
- `templates/path.html` draws it with the section page's own paragraph, `templates/_para.html`'s `para()`, moved out of `section.html` (every section page byte-identical after, checked 2026-10-13). A fold's copy of a paragraph drawn elsewhere has no id, so each paragraph's reference is its id once.
- `tests/test_reading_path.py`. Jesus is the largest: 1,960 paragraphs, 2.45 MB (650 KB gzipped), 0.19 s to render here, about a second for Chrome to lay out.

## Work offline

Since 2026-10-13; deployed 2026-10-14, once Harry had answered (CLAUDE.md §10); called Read offline until 2026-10-14. A reader keeps the whole site in their browser, search and every reading path with it, but not the recordings: the cloud at the header's corner, and its menu (in Settings until 2026-10-14: `DECISIONS.md`, Settings). README, "Work offline".

- **The copy is the site's own pages**, so there is no second renderer. `offline/` asks the app for every page in-process (`offline/site.py`), cuts each at fixed places in `base.html` into the build's shell and the page's own pieces (`offline/pages.py`), keeps a section's rail once per paper, and writes gzipped chunks named by their hashes, with `manifest.json`, into `var/offline/` (`offline/pack.py`, the only writer). The reading paths are 132 MB of the 191 unpacked, in chunks of about a megabyte by letter (`pages.PATHS`), Jesus's in one of its own; the shell names each headword's chunk. Two search databases are cut from `ub.db` (`offline/searchdb.py`), their FTS5 tables declared as `ub.db` declares them, and one for each translation, in its own download.
- **The worker** (`ubsite/static/sw.js`) is registered only by a reader who turns it on, and goes to the network first: online nothing changes, and `/stats` still counts them. `ubsite/static/offline-core.js` joins pages, routes every address as `app.py` does, and runs `search()` again on SQLite's WebAssembly build; `offline.js` runs the cloud's menu (what the copy is, its Download, Update and Remove buttons, the install offer) and tells the cloud what the copy is, loaded only when the menu opens or the worker controls the page. The browser keeps each chunk gzipped as it came (`/offline/gz/<hash>`) and unpacks it when read, so the copy takes the room it took to download (44 MB, not 191). A copy kept before 2026-10-14 has its chunks unpacked under `/offline/have/`: the worker reads them as they are, and an update packs them rather than fetch them again.
- **Listening offline** is in the browser's voice: every section's `/speech/` text is in the copy (ten chunks of twenty papers, apart from the pages, which move with every index edit), and offline `ub.js` `voice()` takes one of the device's own voices (`localService`). Offline, a recording chosen in Settings does not play: `listen.js` says why (`NEEDS_NET`).
- **The gate** (`python -m offline check`) runs that same `offline-core.js` in Node and holds what it makes to the server's, byte for byte: 10,283 stored pages (3,878 of them reading paths), the 1,626 sections' spoken text, 1,859 more addresses (every section with all its paragraphs marked, the not-found pages, the redirects) and 503 searches (`tests/fixtures/offline-queries.txt` and `var/visits.db`'s), and every link; since 2026-10-26, 1,288 searches, in the study notes' list and each translation's too, every one with each translation kept, and those that can list one with each kept alone and none. `tools/offline_e2e.py` drives Chrome through turning it on, reading offline, a copy kept unpacked by the worker before, a stale copy updated, an update that fails, and turning it off.
- **Static URLs carry their content's hash** since then (`app.py asset()`: `/static/ub.css?v=…`), so an offline page draws with its own build's stylesheet and scripts.
- **The translations are each a download of their own** (2026-10-26), chosen in Settings' Offline tab (`DECISIONS.md`). `/tr/P:S` is a frame around every translation's headings, then every one's paragraphs, in Settings' order, so each translation's share of a section is two pieces (`app.py tr_pieces`), kept twenty papers to a chunk (`tr-ud-0`), and `pages.build` fails unless the pieces joined in the frame are the server's fragment. The manifest lists them apart, `translations`, each with a `build` of its own: `chunks`, `build` and `bytes` are the copy without them, so a worker from before neither sees nor fetches them, and `deploy.sh`'s list of chunks to keep names theirs too, or they would be pruned. The worker keeps those chosen in the copy's own cache (`meta.tr`), fetches one with `{cmd: 'get', tr}`, lets one go with `drop`, and counts a kept one's revision as the copy out of date; `offline-core.js` joins those kept, and with none kept sends the frame alone, which the fold reads as not in the copy. The gate holds all 1,626 fragments to the server's, with every translation kept.
- **How the copy grew**, as CLAUDE.md §4 recorded it until 2026-10-26: 10,285 pages (3,877 reading paths, and /changelog and /lists/notes since 2026-10-18, /lists/bibliography since 2026-10-19, /api since 2026-10-24) + 1,626 spoken texts in 417 chunks: 47.6 MB to download and keep, 221 MB unpacked, and each translation a download of its own, chosen by the reader: ud 2.0 MB in 5 chunks, urantia-ru 8.9 MB in 11, each with its database for search (2026-10-26; 0.7 MB in 4 and 3.2 MB in 10 until search reached them, the copy 47.4 MB and 218 MB) (2026-10-25, with the interface in Russian too: 400 chunks, 45.2 MB and 199 MB until then; 2026-10-24, index 0aeae38, with /api; 10,284 at index c0db98c, a headword fewer; 10,285 at index 5e357f6, the Zone of Infinity's path gone; 10,286 pages until then; 394 chunks since 2026-10-18, with the math font of the study notes, 396 since 2026-10-19, with the SBL Greek and SBL Hebrew fonts, 400 since 2026-10-20, with every paragraph's reference in a `.stk`)

## Deploy

`deploy/` holds the systemd unit, the nginx server blocks, the sudoers drop-in and `deploy.sh`, with a README naming the four steps that need root on each host. All have been run on both: the site is live at **https://ubsite.ddns.net** (pi5) and, since 2026-10-14, **https://ubsite.sigmaai.au** (the VPS behind sigmaai.au; Tigran: both stay live). `deploy.sh` builds once and ships the same build to each, pi5 then sigmaai.au, the name it reaches the VPS by; `./deploy/deploy.sh sigmaai.au` deploys to one. `ubsite-sigmaai.nginx` is `ubsite.nginx` but for the name and certificate, held to it by `tests/test_nginx.py`. On the VPS, `sigmaai.au` (Open WebUI, `/library/`, `/tair/`, `/cashreconai/`) is nginx's default by parse order alone, so our block never says `default_server`. A subdomain, not `sigmaai.au/ubsite`, because the app assumes it lives at `/` (links, the worker's scope, 29,716 links stored in `ub.db`). Each host has its own `var/visits.db` and `/stats` (one password). **The database is the artifact**: the three source repos are not on the hosts and do not need to be, so `make data` runs here and `var/ub.db` is shipped.

## The interface in Russian

Since 2026-10-25; ROADMAP item 15, its first step. Every word the site says around the book is in every page in English and Russian, and CSS shows the reader's; the book, its index and appendices, `/changelog`, `/api`'s documentation and `/stats` stay English. Plan: `~/.claude/plans/i-would-like-you-elegant-phoenix.md`.

- **The words** are `ubsite/ru.toml`, one line to a string, keyed by its English: a context after `|` where one English says two things (`"Offline|tab"`), a plural as `{ en = [one, other], ru = [one, few, many] }`, a long one by a name (`"home:edition"`). A key and its words are HTML, with `{n}` or `{n:,}` placeholders. Its head lists the words chosen (index entry «рубрика указателя», subentry «подрубрика», 1955 reading «разночтение 1955 года»). `tests/test_l10n.py` holds every entry to both languages, the same placeholders and the same markup (tags, hrefs, classes), and to being named somewhere.
- **`ubsite/l10n.py`** draws them in the templates: `t()` as `<x-en>…</x-en><x-ru lang="ru">…</x-ru>`, custom elements, never spans (trap 33); `ta()` an attribute, English with `data-ru-<name>` beside it; `tt()` and `ru()` for `<title>` and `<option>`, which hold no elements (`<title data-ru="…">`). `tr()` makes words in both languages, an `L`, which never draws itself, so it cannot reach a page as English alone. The Russian plural rule, numbers (a narrow no-break space from five digits, a decimal comma) and dates («18 октября 2026 г.») by hand. An unknown key fails.
- **The scripts** have `/l10n.js` (`ubsite/l10n.js`, the same rules, `T()`), served by `app.py` with only the entries the scripts name, found by their literal `T('…')`, `T.k`, `T.text`, `T.tr`, `T.a` or `T.attr`, and the voices' names; hashed by what it sends. Loaded before `ub.js`; `sw.js` imports it before `offline-core.js`, which draws search and the not-found pages with it, and `offline/check.mjs` loads the copy's own, so the gate holds those to the server's in both languages.
- **ub.js** puts each `data-ru-*` in place and back (`speak()`), with the book's own marks' labels found by selector: a pilcrow's title, a ring's and a study note's name, stored with the text. One `MutationObserver`, shared with Tooltips, speaks what is added later. JS-made text is `innerHTML = T(…)`, so a flag tap needs no re-render.
- **The language** is the setting `lang` (`settings.py` `KEPT`), `auto` until a flag is tapped, then `en` or `ru`; spared by ↺ (`SPARED`). The head script's `ubLang(r, v)` resolves it to `data-lang="ru"` or nothing, before first paint, and `ubTitle()` sets the tab's title. The flags, after the nav (`.top .langs`), are drawn SVG; on a phone CSS `order` puts them at the end of the name's line.
- **The Cyrillic** is EB Garamond's (OFL 1.1, vendored from TeX Live), subset by `build/fonts.py` to U+0400–045F and № and declared in `ub.css` as two more faces of "UB Goudy" over that range, `size-adjust: 110.5%` to Goudy's x-height. UB Goudy has no Cyrillic. Its gate reads `ru.toml` in both languages and holds `ub.css`'s range to the subset; `offline/check.py` "what it loads" holds every `url()` and script to the copy.
- **`UB_BILINGUAL=0`** draws English alone, as every page was before, byte for byte but the script's address; the tests read pages through `en()` (`tests/test_appendix_pages.py`), which is that.
- The rail's version (`book()["v"]`) hashes its own words too, so a fragment kept from before they changed is not drawn again.
