# Changelog

What has changed in *ubsite*, *The Urantia Book* read together with its index, at https://ubsite.ddns.net and https://ubsite.sigmaai.au. The newest is at the top.

The form follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/). A release will be a tag `vX.Y.Z` on `main`, numbered by [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html): the first number goes up when anything that reads `var/ub.db`, the text API or the class names in a paragraph's stored HTML has to change; the second when something is added; the third for a fix. Until the first release, everything is under Unreleased.

Under each version, Added, Changed, Deprecated, Removed, Fixed and Security are written for readers, one plain sentence to an entry, without Markdown: the site's What's new shows them to a reader whose offline copy is older, each dated by the day its line was written. For developers is for anyone who builds, deploys or reads the database: the schema, the API, what the build needs, and anything that breaks. What's new never shows it.

- The player's paragraph buttons cross into the paper before or after: the one for the paragraph before, pressed twice while a paper's title is being read, goes back to the last paragraph of the paper before, and the one for the next paragraph, at a paper's last paragraph, goes on into the next paper from its title; on a section's own page they do the same at a section's title and last paragraph. They used to stop there.
- A paper's quiz asks its questions in a new order each time you take it, and shows each question's answers in a new order too, but where the order means something, as yes and no, or numbers that rise; what to read again is listed in the paper's order.
- A paper's quiz no longer opens with a line saying how many questions it has and where they come from; Question 1 of 29, as you go, says the count.
- The index is revised from its reading of Paper 60, with nine entries more, among them the first true birds sprung from a reptilian group and not from the flying dinosaurs, the grasses second in importance only to the appearance of man, and the sea serpents' fifty-foot body; thirteen entries are reworded or have their citations put right, volcanoes among them.
- A section's page heads it with the section's title alone: the paper's number, title and author stand over its introduction only, as the whole paper's page has them once and a chapter of the Bible names its book over its first chapter alone; the Bible's own heads are larger too, its trail, a book's title and its chapters' headings.
- More small text is larger, in proportion to the text at any width of the window: a note's sources in the List of study notes and under a chapter of the Bible, the line under a page's heading (on the lists, the appendices, a headword's page and search), the lines on the Bible's page, and the API page's tables and code.
- The home page's tile for Cosmology of Personalities is now Charts, a page with a card for the chart and one for Unit conversions, each showing its chart in miniature.
- In Help, the introduction, the list of chapters, the captions under the examples and the table of keys are larger, in proportion to the text at any width of the window.
- The recordings, Merritt Horn's and Григорий Войнер's, come in a much smaller form, so they start sooner, use less data and take far less room kept offline: Merritt Horn's whole reading is 864 MB instead of 7 GB, Григорий Войнер's 981 MB instead of 4.5 GB. Papers you have already kept stay kept and play as before.
- A paragraph's last word may be hyphenated across its last two lines again, as in the printed book, so that the line before it is no longer stretched to keep the word whole; now and then a short piece of a word ends a paragraph.
- Settings' Listening tab and Help's chapter about listening are now called Audio, as their Russian, Аудио, always was.
- A headword's page no longer repeats the headword as the first line of its list: the subentries start at the left, a step less indented, which a phone shows most; the headword's own citations stand under its heading, and its See or See also under Cross-references, which lists the headword's own first.
- The two flags at the header's corner are one list of languages: closed, it shows the flag of the language in use; a tap opens it, each language named in its own words, which leaves room for full screen and for languages to come.
- On a phone, a tap on any paragraph's line of index entries opens the entries under every paragraph, or folds them all, as the Index line at the top of the page does, and they stay so from page to page, Continue reading included; each box keeps its line, open or folded.
- Where the translations are folded under the paragraphs, the paper's and the section's titles have a fold too, with the other texts' titles in it, opening and folding with the others and at the same tab.

- In parallel columns each column's play button now stands at the start of its paragraph, as the first column's does, with the time the paragraph takes where a recording reads it; the paragraph's reference stays with the first column alone.
- Settings' Translations tab is now Texts, and the English is one of the texts in it: put any text first and it stands where the English stood, the others beside it in columns or folded under it, and hide any of them, the English too; where the first has no paragraph, the next that has it stands in, named.

- The paragraph a link takes you to is tinted for seven seconds and then fades by itself, where until now it stayed tinted until you tapped the text; a paragraph you tap yourself keeps its tint until you tap again, as before.

- A headword citing three paragraphs or more can be read through as one text, in the order of the book or by theme.

### Listening

- Every section in Merritt Horn's reading, from any paragraph, or in the browser's own voice. Playing can carry on through the book, and goes on while the reader moves to other pages.

### Work offline

- The whole site can be kept in the browser, search and the reading paths included, but not the recordings; offline, the browser's voice reads.

### For developers

- `var/ub.db` is built from three LaTeX repositories by `make data` and gated by `make check`; it is what the hosts are sent. The README's "Updating the site" is the procedure, and `CLAUDE.md` the record of decisions.
- Its schema is `build/schema/*.sql`; `PRAGMA user_version` is 1.
- The text API, `/api/v1`, documented at `/api`, is at text version 8.
- Python 3.12, FastAPI and Jinja2, rendered on the server; the offline copy is the site's own pages, held to the server's byte for byte by `make offline`.
- `make epub` made the whole site an EPUB 3, private, until it was retired on October 19, 2026; the tag `epub-last` is the last commit that builds it.
