Reading the index here

Beneath each paragraph stand the index entries that cite it, so that the index works in both directions: from a concept into the text, and from any paragraph back to everything the index says about it. An entry opens its headword in the Index; a citation there opens its paragraph; the reader’s Back returns. Entries that cite a whole section stand once, at its head.

Every paragraph begins with its reference, so a search for 101:4.2 finds it. An asterisk marks a word the first printing of 1955 gave otherwise.
