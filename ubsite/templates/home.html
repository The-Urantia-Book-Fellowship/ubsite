About this edition

The text is the Uversa Press edition of The Urantia Book, published by the Urantia Book Fellowship: {{ stats.papers }} papers in {{ '{:,}'.format(stats.sections) }} sections and {{ '{:,}'.format(stats.paragraphs) }} paragraphs, each with a reference of its own. The words of Jesus are set in red, in {{ '{:,}'.format(stats.redletter_paragraphs) }} paragraphs. Its textual apparatus notes every place where the text differs from the first printing of 1955, {{ stats.variants_1955 }} in all, and gives the 1955 reading beneath each.

The index is the Uversa Press index, revised with numerous corrections for the third edition, 2026, where it is bound together with the text in a single volume.

Its {{ '{:,}'.format(stats.citations) }} references, reaching {{ '{:,}'.format(stats.citation_targets) }} places in the text, compiled and checked over many years, are what make the margins of this reader possible. The printed volume remains the book; this is an additional way to move through it.
